Kitchen with action chunks and closed-loop checkpoint selection

Every controller predicts the next 10 joint-velocity commands at each step (ACT's action chunking), executed through ACT's fixed temporal ensemble, and is trained with an L1 loss on the 17 training demonstrations. The kept checkpoint is the one that completes the most tasks on 5 held-out validation episodes, whose environment seeds are disjoint from the 40 test episodes. The fly decoder stays one linear map from the 68 left front-leg motor neurons. Scores are D4RL normalized (25 per completed task), mean ± sd over training seeds.
